🌎 My National Geographic Project

The National Geographic Society funded my research project that expands on my work on lahars. With my NGS project, I'm using a micro-rain radar to improve rainfall estimates for areas of the volcanoes at higher elevations. As an Explorer, I have also participated in the Society's leadership and diversity workshops having the opportunity to facilitate a session in one of these events.
